good questions

Asked at every market.

Do you print the labels too?

No — I design and prepare everything, then work directly with your printer (or one of the two I trust). Proofs pass across my desk before anything runs, so colours land where they should.

What do you need from me to start?

Samples if you can post them, your jar or bag sizes, and anything legally required on pack (allergens, weights, barcodes). No brand yet? Even better — we start from a blank page.

How many tweak rounds do I get?

Two proper rounds per stage, which is plenty because we agree the direction on paper first. Small nudges after that are free; whole new directions are quoted fairly.

Can you match my existing packets?

Yes — ranges grow. I can design new flavours to sit beside old favourites, or gently tidy the whole family so nothing looks like the odd jar out.

How booked up are you?

I take one project at a time, about four weeks each. The marquee up top says what season I'm booking — write in early if you need shelves stocked by Christmas.

Do I own the artwork?

Completely, once the final invoice is paid — files, fonts licences noted, and a short guide so reorders don't need me (though I'm happy to help).
