01

The question

Most planners ask people to manage the tool before they can manage their day. Folio explores a simpler question: what deserves your attention this week?

02

The direction

I replaced the endless task list with three deliberate priorities, then made the rest of the week visible in a single, editable view.

03

What I learned

A smaller set of choices can make a tool feel more capable. The next step is to test how the weekly view fits people with irregular schedules.
