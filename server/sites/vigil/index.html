FAQ

Asked at 3am, answered now.

How is Vigil different from PagerDuty / Opsgenie?

Routing is only the start. Vigil groups related alerts into one incident, drafts timelines and status updates with AI, and closes the loop with postmortems — so a page becomes a 20-minute fix, not a 3-hour thread.

How long does migration take?

Most teams import schedules, services and escalation policies in under an hour with our PagerDuty / Opsgenie importer, then run both in shadow mode for a week before switching.

Do you support on-call compensation and burnout tracking?

Yes. Load scores show nights paged, interruptions per rotation and override debt, so managers can rebalance before someone burns out.

What happens if Vigil itself goes down?

Pages fail over across regions and to SMS/voice carriers directly. Our status page is hosted on fully separate infrastructure — 99.99% delivery over the last 12 months.

Is my incident data used for training?

No. Team and Enterprise workspaces are never used for model training. Ephemeral AI sessions, scoped tokens, SOC 2 Type II and EU residency available.
