05 — FAQ

Questions, answered.

How does the process work?

Book a free consultation and choose your payment option. We agree on a mood board and page plan, build the first draft, complete the changes, enhancements and bug fixes, then make your website live on your server or our shared server. The full-deposit option includes a refund guarantee; milestone payments do not.

What is included in development?

Design + build, copywriting, plus images, icons and visuals we source. Paid stock or premium licenses, if needed, you purchase separately — flagged upfront.

What are my payment options?

Websites start from ₹10,000, with the final quote confirmed after your free consultation. Option 1: pay the full quoted amount as a deposit, with a full refund guarantee if you don’t love the final website. Option 2: pay a 30% advance, 40% at milestone 1 (the first website draft), and the remaining 30% at final delivery. All payments under option 2 are non-refundable.

What counts as milestone 1 and final delivery?

Milestone 1 is delivery of the first draft of your website; the 40% payment is due then. After that, we complete the changes, enhancements and bug fixes. Final delivery is complete when those are finished and the website is live on your server or our shared server; the remaining 30% is due then.

Can I get a refund with milestone payments?

No. The milestone payment option is non-refundable, including the 30% advance, the 40% first-draft payment and the final 30%. The full refund guarantee applies only to the full amount deposit option.

How does the full refund work?

With the full amount deposit option, if you don’t like the final website after delivery and changes, tell us within 7 days of delivery and we refund the full amount. No lengthy process. This guarantee does not apply to the milestone payment option.

What happens in the planning session?

Mood board for look & feel, plus every section and page planned with you — no surprises later.

Where will my website be hosted?

Your choice — your own hosting with our free setup help, or our shared VPS at ₹3,000/year.

Do I own the website?

Yes. Once you approve the final site it's handed over to you. Domain + any paid licenses stay in your name.

What businesses is this for?

Any business — shops, cafés, clinics, salons, real estate, schools, services, startups. Unsure? Book a free call and we'll tell you honestly.
