Questions, answered.

How does the process work?

First, book a free consultation. If you're ready, pay a small deposit. Then we do a planning session (mood board + section and page plan), build the website (development), deliver it with a round of changes — and if you like it, it's yours. If not, full amount refund.

What is included in development?

Development includes the actual design and build, plus copywriting for the website, plus images, icons and visuals we source for you. If your site needs paid stock or premium licensed content, you purchase that separately — we'll flag it upfront.

How much is the deposit? And the total?

Websites start from ₹10,000 onwards. The exact quote and deposit are confirmed after the free consultation, based on your pages and requirements. The balance is due only if you like the final website.

How does the full refund work?

Simple: if you don't like the final website after delivery and changes, we refund the full amount you paid. No lengthy process, no hard feelings.

What happens in the planning session?

We set up a mood board so you can see the look and feel before we build, and we plan every section and page of the website with you — so there are no surprises later.

Where will my website be hosted?

Your choice. If you want your own hosting or server, we'll help you set it up. Or you can host on our shared VPS for ₹3,000 per year — zero hassle.

Do I own the website?

Yes. Once you've paid in full, the website is handed over to you with hosting of your choice. Domain and any paid licenses you purchased remain in your name.

What kind of businesses is this for?

Any business that needs a website — shops, cafés, clinics, salons, real estate, schools, services, startups and more. If you're unsure, book the free consultation and we'll tell you honestly if it's a fit.
