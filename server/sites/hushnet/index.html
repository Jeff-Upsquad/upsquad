07 — FAQ

Plain answers.

What can you see?

Account number and connecting address while connected. Never destinations or content; connecting addresses are not stored.

How is multihop different?

Most multihop runs both hops. Our exits are independent, so one operator alone cannot link you.

Will it work on filtered networks?

Usually. Traffic resembles ordinary web transport and is harder to fingerprint. Stealth padding helps further.

How many devices?

Five simultaneous. Sign in anywhere; only active connections count.

Router support?

Yes via WireGuard configs. You keep split-trust, without stealth camouflage.

Anonymous payment?

Lightning or cash-by-mail credited to your number. Card stays with the processor, not tied to usage.
